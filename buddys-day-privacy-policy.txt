Privacy Policy for Buddy's Day

Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Buddy's Day ("the App") is developed and published by Erdem Ozdemir.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Buddy's Day does not require user registration and does not create user accounts.

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

This includes, but is not limited to:

Name
Email address
Phone number
Postal address
Photos
Audio recordings
Contacts
Location
Device identifiers
Usage analytics
Crash reports
User-generated content
Data Collection

Buddy's Day does not collect any user data.

All gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device.

No information is transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Buddy's Day does not use third-party services that collect user data.

The App does not include:

Advertising SDKs
Analytics SDKs
Tracking technologies
In-App Purchases
Children's Privacy

Buddy's Day is designed for young children and families.

The App complies with Apple's Made for Kids requirements.

Buddy's Day does not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from children or any other users.

Data Security

Since no personal information is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data stored on developer-managed servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page together with the updated revision date.
