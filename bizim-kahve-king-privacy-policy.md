# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Bizim Kahve: King** ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

The App is developed and published by **Erdem Ozdemir**.

## 1. Information We Collect

Bizim Kahve: King does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play the game.

The App does not operate its own user registration system, social network, or messaging service.

Game-related information, such as game progress, scores, settings, and saved game state, may be stored locally on your device so that you can continue your game. This information is not transmitted to our own servers.

## 2. Advertising

Bizim Kahve: King uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements.

Google AdMob is a third-party advertising service. Depending on your consent, device settings, location, and applicable privacy requirements, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

* Advertising identifiers and device identifiers
* IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
* Device and operating system information
* App and advertising interaction information
* Information related to ad requests, impressions, and interactions
* Diagnostics and performance information associated with advertising services

This information may be used for purposes including ad delivery, ad measurement, fraud prevention, security, and, where permitted and consented to, personalized advertising.

For more information about how Google handles information in connection with advertising services, please see Google's privacy information:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Consent and Privacy Choices

For users in jurisdictions where consent is required, the App uses Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to present privacy and advertising consent choices.

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may be asked to provide or manage consent for purposes such as personalized advertising, advertising-related data processing, and the use of local storage or similar technologies.

You may withdraw or change your consent choices through the privacy controls made available by the App where supported.

Google's privacy and consent practices are described here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 4. App Tracking Transparency

On iOS devices, the App may request permission through Apple's **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)** framework.

This permission is used to determine whether the App may access data for tracking purposes as defined by Apple's privacy framework, including tracking across apps and websites.

You can allow or deny this permission. Denying tracking permission does not prevent you from using the App.

You can change tracking permissions at any time through your iOS device settings.

For more information about Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework:

https://developer.apple.com/documentation/apptrackingtransparency

## 5. Children's Privacy

Bizim Kahve: King is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

The App does not require an account or request a child's name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal information.

Because the App uses third-party advertising services, advertising and privacy treatment may depend on the user's age, location, applicable laws, consent status, and the configuration of the advertising service.

Where required, appropriate privacy and consent mechanisms are used for users subject to applicable children's privacy requirements.

## 6. Local Data Storage

The App may store game progress, game settings, and other game-state information locally on your device.

This information is used to provide gameplay functionality, such as continuing an existing game or remembering application preferences.

We do not operate a server that stores your personal game profile or requires an online account.

Deleting the App may remove locally stored game data, depending on the storage mechanism and your device settings.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not operate a system that collects or shares your personal information with third parties for our own marketing purposes.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob may be shared or processed by those providers according to their own privacy policies, the purposes of the services they provide, and your consent where legally required.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the App does not require a user account and does not maintain a personal user database, we do not retain a personal account profile for you on our own servers.

Game information stored locally on your device remains there until it is deleted by the App, overwritten, or removed through actions such as deleting the App or its stored data.

Information processed by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

For information about Google's data practices, please visit:
